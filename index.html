FAQ
Is there a free trial?
You can create an account and explore the platform immediately. Billing begins when you're ready to unlock full access.
How does the parent dashboard work?
Parents sign up separately and receive a 6-digit link code. Their child enters this code during signup to connect accounts. From then on, the parent dashboard updates in real time.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel from account settings and access continues until end of billing period.
How is Zenith different from Khan Academy?
Zenith uses live AI to explain every answer, adapts to your specific weak spots, and gives parents real-time visibility. It's an interactive practice engine, not a video library.
